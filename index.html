Vision

From isolated clips to coherent film experiences.

Today’s tools can produce impressive still images and striking short videos. Long-form filmmaking demands more: persistent identity, scene memory, directable performance, multilingual dialogue, selective scene regeneration, and a workflow that creators can actually control.

Each project is both a creative work and a test case for the future of AI-native cinema.

Case Study 01

The Father

A contemporary dramatic adaptation centered on power, paternity, public image and collapse. It tests continuity of character, performance direction, speeches, intimate conflict and the ability to revisit a single scene without breaking the whole film.

  • Character continuity
  • Dialogue & lip-sync
  • Corporate and intimate staging
Case Study 02

Memories

A visual and poetic exploration of memory, fear and perception. It tests non-human motion, visual continuity, atmosphere, dream logic and the translation of still images into emotionally coherent cinematic movement.

  • Poetic visual continuity
  • Animal motion
  • Atmosphere & texture
Case Study 03

The missing piece

A rapidly developed dramatic project about retirement, fragility, social bonds and the quiet conflicts that surface when time, identity and future security are thrown into question. It tests ensemble scenes, intimate realism and slow-burn emotional shifts.

Research challenges

What is still missing for AI-native cinema?

CineAI Lab is designed to help developers and researchers see where current systems still fall short when asked to behave like cinema tools rather than clip generators.

Persistent identity

Keep the same character credible across many scenes, angles, emotions and time intervals.

Directable performance

Support pauses, hesitations, looks, breathing, emotional nuance and actor direction beyond literal speech.

Scene memory

Preserve costumes, props, locations and staging across multiple generations.

Selective regeneration

Rebuild scene 12 without accidentally rewriting scenes 3, 8 and 14.

Long-form narrative coherence

Follow dramatic arcs over the duration of a short or feature-length film, not only isolated moments.

Human creative control

Keep authorship, revision and judgment in human hands while benefiting from AI acceleration.
